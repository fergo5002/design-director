# A page can pass every pixel check and still be unreadable

**Kind:** rejection.

A brand site had measured contrast, visible focus, correct reduced motion and true
copy, and its owner still could not read it: 1,036 words over sixteen phone screens,
five prose-only sections in a row. Every gate measured a pixel; none measured load.

**Prevention:** `ddr shots` reports words in `main` and screens tall. A marketing page
over about 400 words of prose, or more than one text-only section in a row, is not
finished. Count words inside a graphic separately: they are part of the picture.
