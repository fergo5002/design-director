# Six materials on one skeleton is not exploration

**Kind:** rejection.

Asked to redesign a mascot, an agent produced six polished builds of the same anatomy
in six materials (paper, wire, wood and so on). All were rejected together: the
variety was surface, the character was unchanged.

**Prevention:** for marks, mascots and characters, diverge first in at least a dozen
cheap sketches that vary the construction itself (proportion, silhouette, face,
gesture, line language), judged side by side at the size they will live at. Build a
polished version only of the one or two that get a reaction.
